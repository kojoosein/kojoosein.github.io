Tandem-Wing eVTOL Design, Simulation, and Flight Testing

Unmanned Aerial Systems Laboratory, Cal Poly Pomona NSF REU Research Intern Complete May 2023 – Aug 2023

I developed tandem-wing eVTOL geometry and detailed components, evaluating wing loading, aircraft balance, and propulsion placement against hover and cruise requirements.

drag to rotate · scroll to zoom

The problem

The eVTOL project was my first full design-build-fly loop, and it taught the difference between a configuration that is geometrically feasible and one that is dynamically controllable. Hover and efficient forward flight want different aircraft; a tandem wing adds interactions between the wings, the propulsion placement, and the transition — and flight testing is where those interactions stop being abstractions.

What I did

  • Developed tandem-wing eVTOL concept geometry in OpenVSP and detailed components in SolidWorks and Fusion 360, evaluating wing loading, center-of-gravity envelope, and propulsion placement against conflicting hover and cruise requirements.
  • Fabricated and assembled prototype structures and integrated motors, servos, control hardware, wiring, and batteries, including machine-shop work on airframe components.
  • Conducted X-Plane and autopilot simulation followed by ground and flight testing, revising the configuration in response to observed stability and control behavior and supporting propulsion performance optimization for the lab's NSF REU program.

What went wrong

A configuration can be geometrically sound and still be difficult to control. Wing interaction, mass distribution, and transition behavior showed up in testing in ways the concept model did not predict.

Onboard Visual Target Tracking for sUAS

Personal project Developer In progress Aug 2026 – Present

I am developing an onboard visual target tracking project that connects target detection and tracking with gimbal commands.

Personal project

The problem

Detection on a laptop is a solved exercise; detection on a flying vehicle is a latency problem wearing a computer-vision costume. What interests me is where the two meet — how much delay the control loop tolerates before tracking turns into oscillation.

What I did

In-progress onboard perception project: detection and tracking running on a Raspberry Pi with an Arducam feed, closing the loop by commanding gimbal or vehicle yaw to hold a target centered. The engineering questions are the embedded ones — inference rate against frame rate, end-to-end latency budget, and what pointing error the loop can actually hold once the airframe is moving.

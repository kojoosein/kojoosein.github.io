Electropulsing Treatment of Grey Cast Iron (Senior Capstone, Team FORTIS)

Howard University Senior Project Team Lead Complete Aug 2025 – May 2026

I led a four-person team investigating electropulsing treatment of grey cast iron, designing the test fixture and specimen geometry and planning the fatigue test campaign.

Howard University

The problem

The capstone forced a different discipline than my UAV work: coupled electrical-thermal-mechanical design where the failure mode of the experiment itself — contact heating, bulk overheating, electrode resistance — could silently masquerade as the effect being studied. I led the team, and the thermal model that separated a crack-tip treatment from simply cooking the specimen was my core engineering contribution, alongside the fixture design and fatigue test plan.

What I did

  • Designed the test fixture and specimen geometry (69.85 x 7.62 mm) in CAD and planned the fatigue campaign using Basquin relationships, predicting 10^6 to 10^7 cycle lives at 1,250 to 1,750 N loads.

Results

Fatigue campaign planned for 10^6 to 10^7 cycle lives at 1,250 to 1,750 N on 69.85 x 7.62 mm specimens

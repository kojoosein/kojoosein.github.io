3D-Printed Scientific Sensor Mounts for a DJI Matrice 100

@FTERLab, Applied Fluids and Thermal Engineering Research Laboratory, Howard University Student Researcher Complete Aug 2023 – Jan 2025

I designed and fabricated mounts for a 753 g scientific payload, accounting for aircraft balance, component clearance, and sensor access during field deployments.

drag to rotate · scroll to zoom

The problem

Mounting a scientific instrument on a multirotor is not a packaging problem. The mount moves the center of gravity, transmits vibration, and parks the sensor inlet in rotor-disturbed air that can bias the measurement itself. My first research project was learning to satisfy the structural, aerodynamic, manufacturing, and measurement requirements simultaneously — and proving in hover tests against stationary references that the integrated system produced scientifically usable data. It became my first first-author publication, at AIAA SciTech 2025.

What I did

  • Designed and 3D printed scientific sensor mounts in SolidWorks and Fusion 360 carrying a 753 g Alphasense OPC-N3 and iMet-XQ2 payload, balancing center of gravity, propeller and landing gear clearance, and access to sensor ports across repeated field deployments.
  • Standardized the same payload across every quadrotor in the lab so measurements were platform agnostic, letting datasets be compared across airframes instead of being tied to a single vehicle.
  • Ran CFD in ANSYS Fluent to position the sensor inlet approximately 0.2 m above the vehicle body across 3,000 to 7,000 RPM rotor speeds and 5 m/s headwind and 2 m/s crosswind cases, so rotor induced turbulence would not bias the particulate measurements the aircraft existed to collect.
  • Validated the integrated system against stationary reference sensors in hover testing, demonstrating research grade measurements from rapidly replaceable printed hardware instead of costly custom machined payload structures.

Results

First-author paper at AIAA SciTech 2025
753 g combined sensor payload integrated and flight-validated

GPS-Denied Visual Navigation Testbed

Personal project Developer In progress Aug 2026 – Present

I am developing a testbed to explore visual position estimation when GNSS is unavailable.

Personal project

The problem

GNSS fails indoors, under canopy, near structures, and wherever someone wants it to. A vehicle that only knows where it is when the sky cooperates is not autonomous — it is supervised. This testbed is how I am learning the estimation side of that problem with hardware I already own.

What I did

In-progress GPS-denied navigation testbed: camera calibration, then AprilTag and ArUco pose estimation, transformed into the vehicle body/NED frame, timestamped, and sent to a Cube Orange as MAVLink vision-position messages with EKF source selection configured to accept them. Later phases add optical-flow odometry with rangefinder scale and drift evaluation over closed paths. Validated on a staged ladder from bench markers to restrained motion to flight.

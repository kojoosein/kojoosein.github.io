Multi-UAV Formation Architecture in Simulation

Personal project Developer Aug 2026 – Present

Leader-follower formation logic proven in simulation first — the interesting questions are about dropout and latency, not geometry.

Hero image drop the file in and rebuild — no code change needed

The problem

The interesting questions in formation flight are not geometric, they are about failure: what happens when a link drops, when a vehicle leaves, when bandwidth will not carry every state. I am deliberately building this in simulation first and refusing to claim flight results I have not earned.

What I did

In-progress multi-vehicle architecture: each aircraft keeps an independent flight controller and safety pilot, a companion computer runs vehicle-level logic, and formation positions are defined as offsets from a leader or virtual leader, with a global enable that prevents accidental activation. Proven in SITL across staged steps — simulated pairs, then one real vehicle following a virtual leader — studying how communication latency and vehicle dropout degrade formation error before any two-aircraft flight.
